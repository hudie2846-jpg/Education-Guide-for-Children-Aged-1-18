### Further reading: understanding, not proof of effectiveness

- [The Book You Wish Your Parents Had Read](https://read.douban.com/ebook/157209563/) · Philippa Perry · United Kingdom. For reflecting on parents’ own emotions and relationship patterns.
- [How to Talk So Little Kids Will Listen](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/How-to-Talk-so-Little-Kids-Will-Listen/Joanna-Faber/The-How-To-Talk-Series/9781501131639) · Joanna Faber and Julie King · United States. The publisher identifies ages 2–7; useful for understanding communication with young children.
- [How to Talk So Kids Will Listen & Listen So Kids Will Talk](https://book.douban.com/subject/37928019/) · Adele Faber and Elaine Mazlish · United States. For practicing listening and making requests clearly.
- [Positive Discipline](https://book.douban.com/subject/3420606/) · Jane Nelsen · United States. For understanding kind, firm boundaries; not all techniques necessarily have the same effects.
- [Positive Discipline for Teenagers](https://my1.zjhzlib.cn/opac/book/2003944758?globalSearchWay=author) · Jane Nelsen and Lynn Lott · United States. For thinking about adolescent autonomy and responsibility.
- [The Whole-Brain Child](https://www.tinabryson.com/books) · Daniel J. Siegel and Tina Payne Bryson · United States. For thinking about emotional guidance. Popular-science metaphors are not diagnoses or laws of neuroscience.
- [家庭教育 — Family Education (descriptive translation)](https://www.cp.com.cn/book/d1accad4-0.html) · Chen Heqin · China. Classic educational observations from an earlier era; assess specific practices against modern knowledge.
- [好妈妈胜过好老师 — A Good Mother Is Better Than a Good Teacher (descriptive translation)](https://jp.tfswufe.edu.cn/opac/book/3c18c1c71fa0c570a0bd1936157b0694) · Yin Jianli · China. Chinese family examples of reading, learning, and communication; fathers can read it too.
- [新家庭教育论纲 — An Outline of New Family Education (descriptive translation)](https://www.hneph.com/10008/books/detail/1333251460464377895) · Zhu Yongxin · China. For considering the aims of family education and family roles.
